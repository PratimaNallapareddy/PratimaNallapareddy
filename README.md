## 🤖 AI Research Intelligence Agent — KPMG × Break Through Tech AI

**Python • NLP • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) • LLMs • Vector Embeddings • Semantic Search**

Collaborating with **KPMG through the Break Through Tech AI Studio** to develop an AI-powered research intelligence agent that analyzes academic research papers, generates citation-supported summaries, and translates complex technical findings into actionable business insights.

Highlights:
- Developing a research pipeline to ingest, process, and organize academic AI research papers
- Implementing **Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)** and semantic search to retrieve relevant research findings
- Leveraging **LLMs and vector embeddings** to support research summarization and business insight generation
- Building evaluation frameworks to assess retrieval accuracy, citation quality, and generated responses
- Collaborating with a cross-functional team to develop an AI solution addressing real-world business challenges
